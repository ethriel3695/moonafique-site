# Monthly membership fulfillment

Updated October 25, 2026. Use [the staff dashboard](https://www.moonafique.com/admin/grimmunity) with an approved Moonafique staff account. See [the current offer](collections-and-shipping.md) for contents, prices, capacities, and package profiles.

## Prepare each month

1. Save the monthly edition schedule in America/Boise time before renewal payments are due.
2. Check the separate tier allocations: Starter uncapped, Keeper 12 plus one retained, ACEO Prints 24, ACEO Originals 12.
3. Prepare the Starter ear option, mystery finish, six emotion faces, two pairs of pants, two boots, and two matching pets.
4. Prepare the Keeper's exclusive paint job and accessories on the Grimm itself. Make 13 Keeper copies, including the retained copy. A stand and separate accessory pack are not promised.
5. Prepare two physical ink prints per ACEO Prints member and one original artwork per ACEO Originals member.
6. Run **Billing checks** and review exceptions. An active subscription or successful checkout screen is not a packing instruction.

Schedules and edition allocations cannot change after a reservation or payment references an edition. Never reopen a vaulted edition to handle a late payment. A missing edition or late recovery requires review.

## Pack a paid order

1. Select the month and review each paid order's saved tier and included contents.
2. For Starter, read the saved preferred colors and colors to avoid. Use the paid order's preferences, even if the member changed their account preferences later.
3. Check delivery details and unresolved payment or address exceptions.
4. Choose **Lock for packing**. The website checks payment and saves the address for this shipment.
5. Export the ready packing list if needed. It includes saved contents and color preferences. Keep address exports private.
6. Weigh the sealed parcel and confirm it fits the package profile before buying postage.

Each membership creates one monthly shipment. A customer with both a Grimm and an ACEO membership has separate paid obligations and shipping charges. Do not combine shipments without arranging the fulfillment and shipping-charge adjustment with the operator.

## Buy postage and dispatch

1. On a ready order, choose **Get Shippo rates**.
2. Select the carrier service and review its displayed cost.
3. Confirm the label purchase. Use a Shippo test key only for sandbox orders.
4. Download and print the 4 × 6 label.
5. Dispatch the parcel, then choose **Mark dispatched** to save its tracking and notify the member when member emails are enabled.

If a purchase times out or needs review, check the order-tagged transaction in Shippo before doing anything else. Do not buy another label to test whether the first purchase worked. Use **Recover existing Shippo label** to attach the matching transaction ID; the website verifies its reference and rate. Ask the operator to resolve a failed purchase with no usable transaction.

Postage purchased elsewhere can be recorded through the manual tracking option when no Shippo purchase is pending or recorded. This does not create or refund postage.

## Address changes

A destination change on a Shippo-rated membership creates a review hold. Confirm the new address and whether the accepted recurring shipping fee is still appropriate. Do not silently increase a member's shipping charge.

Before postage purchase, staff can update delivery details in Stripe, record the correction reason, unlock packing, and lock again. The old packing address remains in the audit history. Unlocking removes any unused label quote. A pending, uncertain, or completed label purchase blocks this correction; resolve that label with the operator first.

## Payment and cancellation

Unpaid, refunded, disputed, or adjusted invoices must not be packed. Review notes acknowledge investigation; they do not release financial holds. **Recheck and release hold** requires a valid fully paid invoice without an active refund or dispute.

Cancellation stops future renewals. Already-paid editions remain due unless refunded. A canceling member keeps the current commitment, so their place cannot be resold into an already-allocated edition. New places become available only when the relevant future allocation allows them.

Keeper's **Vaulted for now** availability message means its twelve places are full. It does not stop paid members receiving future monthly editions. The separate staff **Vaulted** edition state is permanent and requires all paid orders to be shipped or appropriately resolved. Neither action cancels Stripe subscriptions.

Tier switching and automatic rejoining after cancellation are not enabled. Send those requests to the operator; do not manually replace Stripe items.

## Interest list

Interest signup requests launch news, collects no payment, and reserves no place. Use **Export launch interest list** for consented contacts only. Do not add them to a general newsletter without consent. Sending a launch announcement is a separate owner action.
